Cake Decorating

Cake decorating can turn a simple cake into something special. Learning a few basic techniques and using the right tools can make decorating easier and more enjoyable.

Image Source: Insteading

Start With a Cooled Cake

Allow your cake to cool completely before decorating. Frosting a warm cake can cause the frosting to melt and make decorating more difficult.

Apply a Crumb Coat

Spread a thin layer of frosting over the cake before adding the final layer. The crumb coat helps trap loose crumbs so they do not show through the finished frosting.

Chill Before the Final Coat

After applying the crumb coat, chill the cake so the frosting can firm up. This creates a smoother surface for the final layer of frosting.

Use the Right Decorating Tools

A few basic tools can make cake decorating easier:

  • Piping bags
  • Piping tips
  • Offset spatula
  • Cake scraper
  • Turntable

Practice Piping

Before piping directly onto your cake, practice your design on parchment paper or another flat surface. This can help you get comfortable controlling the piping bag and the amount of pressure you use.

Simple Cake Decorating Steps

  1. Allow the cake to cool completely.
  2. Level the cake layers if needed.
  3. Add filling between the layers.
  4. Apply a thin crumb coat.
  5. Chill the cake.
  6. Add the final layer of frosting.
  7. Finish with piping, sprinkles, or other decorations.
